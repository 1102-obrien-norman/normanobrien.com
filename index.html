Services

Search is splitting in two. Your customers use both halves.

Search engine optimization

SEO

Ranking in Google and Bing results. It comes down to a site that's fast, easy to crawl, and answers what people actually search for.

Generative engine optimization

GEO

Being mentioned and linked in AI answers. That means clear, well-structured content and signals that let an AI trust and quote you.

  • Visibility audit

    A plain-language report on how your site performs in search and in AI answers today, what competitors are doing, and the fixes ranked by impact.

  • Technical fixes

    Page speed, indexing, structured data, and site structure. The behind-the-scenes work that lets search engines and AI crawlers understand your pages.

  • Content built to be cited

    Rewriting and planning pages so they answer real customer questions clearly enough to rank, and specifically enough to be quoted.

  • Tracking over time

    Re-running the same searches and AI prompts before and after changes, so you can see what moved and whether it was a real improvement.

AI visibility tracker

In development

Software for my consulting practice

Measures whether AI assistants and search engines recommend a business, and whether that changes after the work is done.

  • Sends the same prompts to ChatGPT, Perplexity, Gemini, Claude, Google's AI Overviews and AI Mode, plus regular Google and Bing results
  • Records whether the business is named, where it appears in the answer, and whether its website is cited
  • Produces a client-ready report with a before-and-after comparison
  • Gives every number a range, so a real improvement can be told apart from the normal run-to-run variation in AI answers
