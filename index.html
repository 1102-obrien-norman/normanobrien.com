Natural Language Processing of Financial Disclosures

Capstone

Senior capstone, Oregon State University. Four-person team with an industry project partner. My role: AI summarization with the Google Gemini API.

A research pipeline that reads thousands of SEC filings so analysts don't have to. It finds the recurring topics across 10-K and 8-K reports, then explains each one in plain English.

  • My part, summarization: built the stage that sends BERTopic's output to the Google Gemini API, iterating through every topic cluster and returning a concise, readable summary of each. Summaries are appended to the final CSV next to topic IDs and scores, and non-English filings get English summaries.
  • Topic modeling: the team used BERTopic, an embedding-based clustering method, to discover topics across 30,000+ filings and map each filing to its most relevant topic and keywords
  • Data: filings pulled through Wharton Research Data Services (WRDS), cleaned of HTML, XBRL, and boilerplate, and split into chunks the model could handle
  • Output: structured CSV files, with an optional DeepL step for more precise, auditable translation

AI visibility tracker

In development

Software for my SEO and GEO consulting work

Measures whether AI assistants and search engines recommend a business, and whether that changes after optimization work.

  • Sends the same prompts to ChatGPT, Perplexity, Gemini, Claude, Google's AI Overviews and AI Mode, plus regular Google and Bing results
  • Records whether the business is named, where it appears in the answer, and whether its website is cited
  • Produces a client-ready report with a before-and-after comparison
  • Gives every number a range, so a real improvement can be told apart from the normal run-to-run variation in AI answers
